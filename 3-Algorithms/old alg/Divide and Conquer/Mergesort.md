#coding/algorithm 
split array into two equal size subarrays, sort these subarrays with a recursive call, and shuffle these two sorted subarrays together by managing two pointers across the subarrays inserting the lesser of the two values

condition: the subarrays must be sorted for the merging function to work correctly.
Benefit: runs in nlogn time due to the divide and conquer theorem
